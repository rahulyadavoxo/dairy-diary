Privacy Policy

Dairy Diary · Owner and operator: Rahul Kumar, India · Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Dairy Diary app ("the app", "we") collects, why, where it is stored and the choices you have. It is written for dairy owners who use the app ("you") and the customers whose details a dairy enters.

सार (हिंदी): ऐप सिर्फ़ वही जानकारी रखता है जो डेयरी खुद डालती है — ग्राहकों के नाम, फ़ोन नंबर, दूध/पनीर की एंट्री, रेट और पेमेंट — ताकि रजिस्टर, हिसाब और बिल बन सकें। ये जानकारी Google Firebase पर सुरक्षित रहती है, सिर्फ़ उसी डेयरी के जोड़े गए फ़ोन देख सकते हैं। हम ये जानकारी किसी को बेचते नहीं, विज्ञापन नहीं दिखाते। डेटा हटवाने के लिए ईमेल करें।

1. Information we collect

  • Account: the dairy owner's email address and password (the password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see it). Phones that join a dairy get an anonymous account ID.
  • Dairy data you enter: dairy name, phone number and UPI ID; customer names, phone numbers, areas and notes; daily milk and paneer entries, rates, bills and payments; names you give to phones.
  • Device and app data: app version, and whether notifications and battery settings are allowed (to make reminders work). Crash or error details may be kept on the phone to help fix problems.

We do not collect location, contacts lists, photos from your gallery, or advertising IDs.

2. How we use it

  • To run the app: keep the register, calculate the monthly hisaab, make bill images and sync the dairy's data between its phones.
  • To send bills to customers on WhatsApp. Today the app opens WhatsApp and you press Send yourself. If you turn on automatic bills in future, bills will be sent through the WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta) from your dairy's own WhatsApp Business number, only to customers you have added and who have agreed to receive them.
  • To show reminders (for example, to fill the register).

We do not sell, rent or share your data for advertising.

3. Where data is stored and who can see it

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Authentication), operated by Google LLC, and cached on your phones so the app works offline. Each dairy's data can be read and changed only by the phones that dairy has added; other dairies cannot see it. When automatic WhatsApp bills are used, the customer's phone number and bill are passed to Meta Platforms to deliver the message, under Meta's own terms and privacy policy.

4. Your customers' data

The dairy owner decides what customer information to enter and is responsible for having the customers' permission to keep it and to send them bills on WhatsApp. Customers can ask their dairy, or us, to correct or remove their details.

5. How long we keep it

Data is kept while the dairy uses the app. When a dairy asks us to delete its account, we delete its account and dairy data from our database within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep something.

6. Your rights and choices

You can view and edit your data in the app, remove phones from your dairy, and ask us for a copy, correction or deletion of your data by emailing us. We follow applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

7. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections and is protected by access rules so that only a dairy's own phones can reach it. An optional PIN protects totals and sensitive settings on a phone. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your password private.

8. Children

The app is meant for business use by adults and is not directed at children under 18.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version; important changes will be shown in the app.
